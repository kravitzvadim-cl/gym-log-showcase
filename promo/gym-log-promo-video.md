# Gym Log: Promo Video Brief

A 55-second vertical promo (plus a 45-second cut) showing two lifters using the Gym Log app through a real training session. Written to be handed to a motion / video-generation AI shot by shot.

---

## 1. Overview

| | |
|---|---|
| **Length** | 55 s master · 45 s cut-down (see section 8) |
| **Format** | 9:16 vertical, 1080×1920, 24 fps (also works cropped to 4:5) |
| **Tone** | Confident, focused, a little cinematic. Real training, not a fitness ad cliché. |
| **Story** | One morning session: open the app → get today's plan and exact weights → lift → log with a tap → rest timer → swap a busy machine → hit a PR → cardio and mobility → session complete → progress and share with a coach. |
| **Tagline** | **Train heavy. Log smart.** |

---

## 2. Characters (keep identical in every prompt)

**DANIEL**: male, 36, experienced powerlifter build (broad shoulders, thick back, strong legs, not a bodybuilder). Short dark hair, neat short beard, focused expression. Wears a charcoal fitted T-shirt, black training shorts, black flat lifting shoes, a lifting belt for heavy sets, a simple black watch.

**MAYA**: female, early 30s, athletic and strong (visible shoulder and leg definition). Dark hair in a high ponytail, light freckles. Wears a deep-blue sports bra under an open grey cropped zip top, black high-waist leggings, white trainers.

They are training partners: friendly, a nod and a quick high-five, no dialogue.

**The phone**: a modern black smartphone, no visible brand logos. The screen always shows the Gym Log app (replaced in edit; see section 6).

---

## 3. Look & location

- **Location**: a modern commercial gym early in the morning. Black rubber floor, steel squat racks, calibrated plates in competition colours (red 25 kg, blue 20 kg, yellow 15 kg, green 10 kg), cable stations, a leg press and hack squat, treadmills by a window, a stretching area with mats.
- **Light**: low warm sunlight through tall windows plus cool overhead strip lights. Visible chalk dust in the light beams. Contrasty, not over-bright.
- **Colour grade**: deep charcoal blacks, clean whites, and the plate colours as the only strong accents (red, blue, yellow, green), matching the app.
- **Camera language**: handheld with stabilisation for energy, a few locked-off hero shots, slow motion (48–60 fps conformed) on the big lifting moments, macro inserts on chalk, hands and the phone.
- **Avoid in every shot**: visible brand logos, text on clothing, mirrors showing the crew, extra people in focus, distorted hands or fingers, gibberish text on screens (screens are replaced in edit).

---

## 4. Music & sound

**Music**: an original instrumental (use a licensed or royalty-free track). Modern hip-hop / electronic, **around 100 BPM**: deep sub bass, crisp claps, a dark synth hook. No vocals, or only short vocal chops.

| Time | Music cue |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:06 | Intro: filtered beat, low rumble, chalk clap lands on the first downbeat |
| 0:06–0:14 | Build: hi-hats in, filter opens |
| **0:14** | **Drop** on Daniel's squat (full beat, bass hits) |
| 0:14–0:32 | Main groove; hard accent on the PR at 0:30 |
| 0:32–0:40 | Breakdown: half-time feel for cardio and mobility, airy pads |
| **0:40** | Second drop: session complete |
| 0:47–0:55 | Outro hook, ends on a single hit and short reverb tail with the logo |

**Sound design** (layered under the music): chalk clap, bar settling in J-hooks, plate clang, belt click, exhale on the lift, soft UI "tick" on every tap, a light whoosh when the rest timer slides in, a short three-beep timer alert, a shimmer on the PR badge.

---

## 5. Shot list: 55-second master

Each shot has: **time**, **shot**, **action**, **phone screen** (what's replaced in edit), **caption** (on-screen text), **sound**, and a **prompt** ready for the motion AI.

**Captions** style: bold condensed uppercase (Big Shoulders Display, or similar), white with a thin shadow. Kept inside the safe area: lower third, or upper third when hands are low. On screen about 1.5–2.5 s each, with a quick slide-up-and-fade animation.

Warm window light, cool overhead lights, cinematic, shallow depth of field.

### Shot 5 · 0:13–0:17 · The squat (DROP)
- **Shot**: Low-angle hero shot, slow motion. Daniel, belted, unracks and squats to depth, then drives up. The music drops exactly as he hits the bottom.
- **Caption**: none. Let the lift breathe.
- **Sound**: Belt click, controlled exhale, bar rattle, **music drop**.
- **Prompt**:
  > Low-angle hero shot, slow motion: a strong 36-year-old man with a short beard and a lifting belt performs a heavy barbell back squat in a steel rack. He descends to full depth, pauses for a split second, and drives up powerfully. Chalk dust floats in golden backlight, red and yellow plates on the bar, intense focused face. Cinematic, 24 fps feel, deep blacks, dramatic rim light.

### Shot 6 · 0:17–0:21 · Tap to log, rest timer starts
- **Shot**: Close-up. Daniel, breathing hard, taps the ✓ on his phone, which rests on the bench. Push in slightly.
- **Phone screen**: The ✓ button fills red with a circular sweep and a checkmark draws in. The rest timer bar slides up from the bottom: *Rest 2:59 · Back Squat*, with a ring counting down.
- **Caption**: `TAP. LOGGED.` then `REST TIMER STARTS ITSELF.`
- **Sound**: UI tick, whoosh as the timer slides in.
- **Prompt**:
  > Close-up of a sweaty man's chalked finger tapping a smartphone lying on a black gym bench, slow push-in. Out-of-focus barbell and red plates in the background, heavy breathing implied, warm side light. Cinematic, shallow depth of field.

### Shot 7 · 0:21–0:25 · Every exercise explained
- **Shot**: Maya at a leg extension machine, mid-rest, swipes up a panel on her phone and reads. Then a quick insert of the screen.

  > Medium close-up of an athletic woman in her early 30s with a high dark ponytail, deep-blue sports top and grey cropped zip top, sitting on a leg extension machine in a modern gym. She reads her smartphone attentively and nods slightly, then looks up ready. Cool blue overhead light mixed with warm window light, cinematic, shallow depth of field.

### Shot 8 · 0:25–0:28 · Machine taken? Swap it

She checks her phone, taps once, smiles slightly, and walks toward a leg press machine. Natural handheld movement, cinematic morning light.

### Shot 9 · 0:28–0:32 · New PR

## 6. App screens to record (screen replacement)

Motion AI can't reproduce the real interface reliably. Record these on an iPhone (Settings → Control Centre → Screen Recording) and composite them onto the phone in each shot. Use **dark mode** (sun/moon button at top right) to match the grade. Log a few sessions beforehand so the numbers look real.

| Shot | What to record in the app |
|---|---|
| 2 | Open the app on a **Monday** → Today screen with the Lower A card |
| 3 | Swipe or tap along the day strip Mon → Sun |
| 4 | Scroll to **Back Squat** → target box with plates; tap **Warm-up sets** |
| 6 | Tick the top set ✓ → rest timer slides up |
| 7 | On **Leg Extension**, tap **How to** → scroll through Benefits, Steps, Tips |
| 8 | On Thursday's **Hack Squat** tap **Swap** → choose **Leg Press** |
| 9 | Enter a deadlift top set heavier than before → tick ✓ → yellow PR badge |
| 10 | Tap **Start workout** → the bottom bar shows the workout time; tap **Start** on Easy cardio |
| 11 | Sunday → Flow section: tap **Round 1**, start a stretch timer, let it switch to Side 2 |
| 12 | Tick the last item of a session → plate spin, sparks, "Session complete" toast, summary card |
| 13a | Progress → Overview → lift tile → chart drawing in |
| 13b | Progress → **Records** |
| 13c | Log → **Export PDF for your trainer** → Create PDF → share sheet |

Tip: show touches in the recordings (or add a soft white touch dot in the edit) so viewers can follow the taps.
